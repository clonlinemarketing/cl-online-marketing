SEO GUIDE

A clearer local presence starts with the basics.

Make your services and business details easier for nearby customers to understand.

Keep business information consistent

Check your business name, contact information, service areas and opening hours on your website and Google Business Profile. Update information when the business changes.

Explain the services you actually provide

Use clear descriptions of what you do and where you work. Useful location information is more valuable to a customer than repeating place names without context.

Make your website useful on mobile

Many local decisions happen on a phone. Make it easy to read your services, check the relevant location and contact your business.

Build a habit of maintenance

Review business details regularly and respond professionally to genuine customer feedback. A local presence needs care as your services, hours and customer needs evolve.
