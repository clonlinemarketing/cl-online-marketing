DIGITAL STRATEGY

Before you spend on ads, check your website.

A practical checklist for turning campaign visits into useful enquiries.

Start with the message

When someone arrives from an ad, can they immediately see the service they searched for? A focused headline and clear explanation help visitors decide whether they are in the right place.

Make the next step obvious

Keep your enquiry options easy to find on mobile. Ask for the information you need to start a useful conversation, and explain what happens next.

Check the complete journey

Open the landing page on a phone. Check loading, readability, navigation and contact links. A campaign cannot compensate for a broken enquiry path.

Measure the actions that matter

Agree what counts as a useful conversion before launching. Review enquiries and lead quality alongside campaign clicks so the next improvement has a clear purpose.
